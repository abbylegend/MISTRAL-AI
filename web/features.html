Feature Modules

Separated tools as dedicated features inside Key Studio.

1) Scene-Aware Beat Frequency Adapter

Upload stems, detect BPM/key, retune frequencies by scene profile.

2) Scene Dashboard + Pop-up Selector

Instant scene switching with live preview and A/B compare.

3) Custom Scene Settings

Configure EQ, compression, reverb, saturation, LUFS targets.

4) Audio Recording Workspace

Browser recording, auto gain/noise reduction, take comping.

5) Vocal Split + Stem Intelligence

Lead/backing split and per-stem issue isolation.

6) Auto Bass-Vocal Generator

Generate low-register support layers matched to phrasing.

7) Multi-Lingual Vocal Generator

Create cross-language performances preserving melodic contour.

8) Retake Sync + Auto Lingual Alignment

Resync language layers after retakes with drift correction.

9) Mastered Vocal Blend + Share

Auto-clean chain and export presets by distribution channel.

10) Prompted Auto Video Generator

Prompt-to-scene video with beat-cut timing by song section.

11) Auto Caption Video Generation

Timed captions, style templates, and subtitle exports.

12) Animated Kids Version Generator

Family-safe animated variant with child-friendly text filtering.

13) Agent Modding + Key Seed + Fork Hosting

Allow safe AI-agent edits, deterministic key seeds, and one-click forking/hosting.
